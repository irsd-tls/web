## General Mission

The **"Organoids" platform** of the IRSD recreates *in vitro* and in three dimensions mini-epithelial organs from **stem cells** isolated from human or murine tissues. These structures are called organoids.

We cultivate organoids derived from human **colons** and **ileums** (collections: COLIC DC-2015-2443 and currently CAPITOL No. RC31/21/0038, in collaboration with hospitals in Toulouse) from healthy, inflammatory (Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis), or tumoral tissues, as well as from murine tissues.

Other cellular models, such as **bladder organoids**, are currently under development.

---

## Models

- **Human colorectal organoids** (colon, ileum, cecum)
  - Healthy
  - Pathological
    - Chronic inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD) (Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis)
    - Colorectal cancers (under development)
- **Murine colorectal organoids** (colon, ileum, cecum)
  - Healthy
  - Inflammatory
- **Human bladder organoids** (under development)

## Services Offered

- Access to various **biobanks** established over several years (tissues, crypts, organoids)
- **Training, collaboration, or service provision** for organoid cultures (L2 level): culture monitoring, immunofluorescence, thawing, passaging, maintenance, and freezing
- Transcriptional and protein expression studies
- Development and implementation of experimental protocols
- Use of a **high-content screening imaging system (Opera Phenix, PerkinElmer)**: Training, collaborations, or services available (acquisition, analysis). [More](people/astrid.canivet).

Recently, we had the immense honor of becoming the holder of the **European biobank of rectal organoids from 47 cystic fibrosis patients**, thanks to the organization *"Vaincre La Mucoviscidose"* (VLM).

## Certifications and Network

Since **2023**, the organoids platform has been labeled **IBISA** (Infrastructures en Biologie, Santé et Agronomie,  [https://www.ibisa.net/](https://www.ibisa.net/)). This certification considers the platform's openness to academic laboratories and private companies, training, technological advancements, and the identification of dedicated personnel with a quality management approach.

It has also been part of the **Toulouse Imaging Network (TRI)** since 2023, with its high-content screening confocal microscope (Opera Phenix) integrated into this network.

You can also find the organoids platform on **LabConnect** ([https://lab-connect.univ-toulouse.fr/](https://lab-connect.univ-toulouse.fr/)).
